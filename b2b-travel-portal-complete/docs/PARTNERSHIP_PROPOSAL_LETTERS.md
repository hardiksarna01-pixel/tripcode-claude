1. IATA Foundation in Travel and Tourism
2. IATA Travel and Tourism Consultant
3. IATA Fares and Ticketing

5. IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations

MARKET OPPORTUNITY:
India's travel industry is growing at [X]% annually. With [X] million travel agents and growing demand for certified professionals, we see significant potential for IATA training programs.

INVESTMENT COMMITMENT:

• Dedicated training facilities

We request a meeting to discuss the partnership terms and ATC application process.

Please find attached:
1. Company Profile
2. Training Infrastructure Details
3. Faculty CVs
4. Financial Statements

Looking forward to your positive response.

Regards,

[Name]

## Partnership Checklist

Before approaching any organization:

- [ ] Company registration documents ready
- [ ] GST and PAN certificates
- [ ] Training infrastructure documentation
- [ ] Faculty CVs and certifications
- [ ] Financial statements (2-3 years)
- [ ] Company profile/brochure
- [ ] Training track record/testimonials
- [ ] Clear partnership proposal with revenue model
- [ ] Follow-up schedule prepared
